Bentonville FAQ

Common Questions

Do you work with supplier and vendor offices in Bentonville?

Yes — it is the single most common type of building we are called into here. Those offices usually need day-porter coverage alongside the nightly scope, because a floor that hosts outside meetings all day will not hold on an overnight-only program.

Can you clean sample rooms and showroom space?

Yes, with a written protocol covering what gets cleaned, what gets moved, and what nobody touches. We set that before the first service rather than after something goes missing.

Can you cover a new office before we move in?

Yes. We handle the post-construction and final detail clean on a build-out and roll straight into the ongoing program, which is considerably cleaner than sorting out cleaning after your team has already moved in.
