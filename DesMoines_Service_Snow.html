Snow & Ice FAQ

Common Questions

When should we lock in a snow contract?

Late summer to early fall. Routes fill up, and a property that signs in December is competing for crews that are already committed elsewhere.

What triggers a plow visit?

A defined accumulation threshold set in your contract — commonly one to two inches. Walks and entries are usually set to a lower trigger than the lot because that is where the liability sits.

Do you document each visit?

Yes — time-stamped service records for every event. In a slip-and-fall claim, documentation of what was done and when is often the whole case.
