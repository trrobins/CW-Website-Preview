Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Philadelphia Commercial Cleaning

How do I get a quote for commercial cleaning in Philadelphia?

Fill out the quote form on this page or call (610) 947-1970. We will schedule a facility walkthrough and build a customized program around your building, schedule, traffic patterns, and priorities.

Do you offer after-hours and weekend janitorial services?

Yes. We schedule daytime, evening, overnight, and weekend service around your operations, whether you are a medical suite in the Northeast, a corporate office off Route 1, or a distribution facility on the I-95 corridor.

Do you do commercial window cleaning as well as janitorial?

Yes, and it is one of the services we are asked for most in this market — office window cleaning on a recurring cycle, storefront and entry glass, multi-story corporate exteriors with the appropriate access method, and full building envelope cleans. It runs inside the same agreement and the same manager relationship.

Do you handle snow and ice for commercial properties?

Yes — lot plowing, walk and entry clearing, pre-treatment ahead of a forecast event, ice melt on a defined trigger, and time-stamped service documentation. Snow routes are committed before the season, so it is worth arranging in late summer or early fall.

What industries do you serve?

Medical offices and healthcare, hospitals and health systems, warehouse and distribution, commercial offices, property management, schools and universities, manufacturing and industrial, banks and credit unions, auto dealerships, churches and religious facilities, daycares, and fitness centers.

Do you serve towns outside Philadelphia?

Yes — Bensalem, Bristol, Levittown, Langhorne, Warminster, Feasterville-Trevose, Morrisville, and communities throughout Bucks County and eastern Montgomery County. Use the ZIP checker to confirm your location.

What sets City Wide apart from other Philadelphia cleaning companies?

Accountability and local presence. City Wide assigns a dedicated Facility Solutions Manager to your account, so one person owns coordination, inspections, communication, and follow-through instead of leaving you to chase multiple contacts.
