Why Wichita Facilities Are Consolidating Vendors Under One Facility Solutions Manager

Ask any Wichita facility manager where their week goes and vendor coordination is near the top of the list. One company cleans, another does floors, a third handles the windows, and a fourth shows up for snow. Every one is a separate contract, invoice, and phone number. A growing number of Greater Wichita businesses are trading that patchwork for a single point of contact — and getting time back in the process.

The hidden cost of a dozen vendors

The problem with juggling multiple building-service vendors isn't just the invoices — it's the coordination tax. Every vendor has its own schedule, its own quality standard, and its own idea of what's in scope. When something falls through the cracks, you're the one making the calls. Multiply that across a busy facility and vendor management quietly becomes a part-time job nobody signed up for.

It also creates blind spots. When the floor company and the janitorial company don't talk, small problems — a recurring spill, a worn entryway, a restroom that's always short on supplies — never get owned by anyone. They just keep happening.

What single-source facility management actually means

City Wide's model is built around one role: the Facility Solutions Manager (FSM). Your FSM is a single, local point of contact who coordinates every building service you use — nightly janitorial, floor care, window cleaning, day porters, disinfection, and seasonal snow & ice. Instead of managing vendors, you manage one relationship.

That FSM knows your building — its traffic patterns, its problem areas, its schedule. When you need something added, changed, or fixed, it's one call. When there's an issue, one person owns it end to end.

Why it works especially well in Wichita

Wichita's business base — aerospace and manufacturing, healthcare, education, distribution, and financial services — tends to run complex facilities with real compliance and scheduling demands. Those are exactly the buildings where a fragmented vendor approach breaks down fastest, and where a single accountable contact pays off most. Whether you run one office downtown or a dozen sites across Sedgwick County, one FSM can coordinate them all.

The bottom line

Consolidating under one Facility Solutions Manager doesn't mean giving up choice — it means giving up the coordination headache. You get consistent quality, one invoice, one contact, and a partner who's accountable for the whole building instead of one slice of it. For a lot of Wichita facilities, that's the difference between managing vendors and running their business.
