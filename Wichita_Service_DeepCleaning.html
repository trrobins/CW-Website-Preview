Deep Cleaning FAQ

Common Questions

How is deep cleaning different from our nightly service?

Nightly service handles daily upkeep; deep cleaning is a periodic detail of the areas that accumulate over time — high dust, appliances, vents, and behind-equipment spaces.

How often do Wichita facilities schedule deep cleans?

Quarterly is common, with some facilities doing it seasonally or twice a year depending on traffic and industry.

Can deep cleaning target just one area, like the break room?

Yes — it can be a whole-facility reset or focused on specific problem areas.
