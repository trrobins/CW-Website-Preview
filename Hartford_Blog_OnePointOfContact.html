One Point of Contact Means Fewer Facility Problems in Greater Hartford

Published August 8, 2026 · 3 min read · City Wide Facility Solutions – Greater Hartford

Managing a facility in Greater Hartford is hard enough without chasing five different vendors for cleaning, floor care, snow, and repairs. When something goes wrong, you're the one making calls, comparing invoices, and hoping the right company shows up.

City Wide is built on a simpler idea: one local point of contact for every service your building needs. Instead of managing a roster of contractors, you manage one relationship — with a Facility Solutions Manager who knows your building.

The hidden cost of juggling vendors

Every extra vendor adds coordination time, another invoice, another point of failure, and another gap where responsibility gets blurry. When the janitorial company and the floor-care company both think the other handled the entrance mats, no one did — and that's how problems (and hazards) slip through.

How single-source facility management works

With City Wide of Greater Hartford, your dedicated manager coordinates the whole program across our full range of services:

  • Recurring commercial janitorial cleaning, night or day
  • Floor care, restroom supplies, and day porters
  • Snow and ice management through the Connecticut winter
  • Handyman and building maintenance for the small repairs that pile up

One call, one contact, one accountable partner — whether you run an office, medical building, school, or warehouse anywhere from West Hartford to Manchester, New Britain, and Bristol.

Why it matters: a single point of contact means faster response, no finger-pointing between vendors, and one partner who already knows your building's history when something needs attention.

Local management, backed by a proven system

You get the responsiveness of a local team that lives and works in Greater Hartford, backed by City Wide's national platform and process. It's the best of both — a neighbor who answers the phone, and a system that scales.
