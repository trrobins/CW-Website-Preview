Financial & Corporate FAQ

Common Questions

Are your crews background checked?

Yes. Personnel assigned to financial and corporate clients are background checked, and we keep the assigned team consistent rather than rotating unfamiliar people through a secure floor.

Can you work around a clean-desk policy?

Yes, and it is a specific part of training for these accounts. Crews clean around documents and never move, stack, or discard paper on a desk or in a work area.

Can you service a trading or dealing floor?

Yes, with a scope and schedule agreed in advance. Those floors have equipment and cabling that cleaning crews must not disturb, and the work is timed to a window your operations team defines.

Our headcount changed with hybrid work. Can the scope change too?

Yes, and it usually should. A lot of Fairfield County offices are still buying five nights a week on a floor plate that is genuinely occupied three. We will rebuild the schedule around actual occupancy.
