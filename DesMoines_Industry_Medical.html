Medical FAQ

Common Questions

Are your crews trained for clinical environments?

Yes — bloodborne pathogen awareness, PPE use, product and dwell-time discipline, and clear boundaries on what they do not touch in an exam or treatment area.

Do you handle regulated medical waste?

We handle general waste and follow your protocol for separation and staging of regulated waste. Licensed medical waste disposal stays with your contracted disposal vendor.

Can you clean between patients rather than after hours?

Yes — day porter coverage for room turnover during clinic hours is common for higher-volume practices.
