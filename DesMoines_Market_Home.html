Common Questions About Des Moines Commercial Cleaning

How do I get a quote for commercial cleaning in Des Moines?

Fill out the quote form on this page or call (515) 329-1499. We'll schedule a facility walkthrough and build a customized program around your building, schedule, traffic patterns, and priorities.

Do you offer after-hours and weekend janitorial services?

Yes. City Wide Des Moines offers flexible scheduling — daytime, evening, overnight, and weekend options — built around your operations, whether you're downtown, in the western suburbs, or out along the interstate corridors.

Do you handle snow and ice for commercial properties?

Yes — lot plowing, walk and entry clearing, ice melt on a defined trigger, and time-stamped service documentation. Snow contracts are set before the season, so it is worth arranging in late summer or early fall.

What industries does City Wide Des Moines serve?

Insurance and financial offices, commercial offices, hospitals and medical facilities, schools and universities, manufacturing and ag-industrial, warehouse and distribution, property management, banks and credit unions, auto dealerships, churches, daycares, and fitness centers.

Do you serve towns outside Des Moines?

Yes — West Des Moines, Urbandale, Ankeny, Ames, Johnston, Clive, Waukee, and the wider central Iowa region including Marshalltown, Newton, Pella, Indianola, and Boone. Use the ZIP checker to confirm your location.

What sets City Wide apart from other Des Moines cleaning companies?

Accountability and local presence. City Wide assigns a dedicated Facility Solutions Manager to your account, so one person owns coordination, inspections, communication, and follow-through instead of leaving you to manage multiple contacts.
