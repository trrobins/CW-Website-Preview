Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Southwest Connecticut Commercial Cleaning

How do I get a quote for commercial cleaning in Stamford?

Fill out the quote form on this page or call (203) 957-8700. We will walk the facility with you, then put together a program shaped around your building, your hours, your traffic, and whatever your current provider keeps missing.

Do you offer after-hours and weekend janitorial services?

Yes. City Wide Southwest Connecticut offers flexible scheduling — daytime, evening, overnight, and weekend options — built around your operations, whether you are in a downtown tower, a Route 7 office park, or a shoreline retail corridor.

Do you handle snow and ice for commercial properties?

Yes — lot plowing, walk and entry clearing, pre-treatment, ice melt on a defined trigger, and time-stamped service documentation. Coastal storms and freeze-thaw cycles make the post-storm visit as important as the plow, and contracts are set before the season.

What industries does City Wide Southwest Connecticut serve?

Financial services and corporate offices, commercial offices, medical and healthcare, hospitals, property management, schools and universities, warehouse and distribution, manufacturing, auto dealerships, churches, daycares, and fitness centers.

Which towns do you serve?

Stamford, Greenwich, Norwalk, Bridgeport, Danbury, Milford, and New Haven, plus Westport, Darien, New Canaan, Wilton, Fairfield, Trumbull, Shelton, Stratford, Hamden, Branford, and the surrounding shoreline communities. Use the ZIP checker to confirm your location.

What sets City Wide apart from other Connecticut cleaning companies?

Accountability and local presence. City Wide assigns a dedicated Facility Solutions Manager to your account, so one person owns coordination, inspections, communication, and follow-through instead of leaving you to manage multiple contacts.
