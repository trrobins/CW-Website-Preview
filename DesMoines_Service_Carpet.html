Carpet Cleaning FAQ

Common Questions

How often should office carpet be cleaned?

Most Des Moines offices do a full extraction one to three times a year, with interim encapsulation and traffic-lane work in between. Buildings with heavy winter entry traffic usually need more.

Will the carpet be dry before we open?

With encapsulation, yes — typically within a couple of hours. Full extraction is scheduled for a Friday evening or a weekend when we want maximum dry time.

Can you get salt stains out?

Usually. Salt leaves an alkaline residue that a standard vacuum will not touch; it needs a neutralizing treatment and extraction. The sooner it is addressed the better the result.
