Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Northwest Arkansas Commercial Cleaning

How do I get a quote for commercial cleaning in Northwest Arkansas?

Fill out the quote form on this page or call (479) 351-0331. We'll schedule a facility walkthrough and build a customized program around your building, schedule, traffic patterns, and priorities.

Which cities do you cover?

Fayetteville, Springdale, Rogers, Bentonville, and Siloam Springs across the corridor, plus Fort Smith and Van Buren to the south — all run by the same team. Surrounding communities including Lowell, Bella Vista, Centerton, Cave Springs, Farmington, Prairie Grove, Pea Ridge, Tontitown, Alma, and Greenwood are covered as well.

Do you offer after-hours and weekend janitorial services?

Yes. City Wide Northwest Arkansas offers flexible scheduling — daytime, evening, overnight, and weekend options — built around your operations, whether you are in a corporate office, a clinic, or a plant running continuous shifts.

Do you handle winter ice and snow for commercial properties?

Yes, and it is scoped for this climate specifically. Freezing rain is the real risk here rather than deep snowfall, so the program leads with pre-treatment ahead of forecast events, walk and entry safety on a lower trigger than the lot, refreeze checks, and time-stamped service documentation. Winter coverage is arranged before the season.

What industries does City Wide Northwest Arkansas serve?

Corporate and supplier offices, warehouse and distribution, food processing and manufacturing, medical and outpatient facilities, hospitals, schools and universities, property management, banks and credit unions, auto dealerships, churches, daycares, and fitness centers.

What sets City Wide apart from other cleaning companies here?

Accountability and coverage. City Wide assigns a dedicated Facility Solutions Manager to your account, so one person owns coordination, inspections, communication, and follow-through — and because we run the whole region as one operation, a business with sites in four cities still deals with one contact.
